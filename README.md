One word for an ex: Intern — temporary, unpaid, and never promoted.

## The word

> Intern

The joke is in the job title: an intern is a trial placement, not a real position — unpaid, temporary, and let go the day the placement runs out. So was the ex. They were never a partner; they were only ever an intern, and the relationship ended the way internships end: no full-time offer, no farewell party, just a lanyard handed back at the door. It lands without a caption, because everyone has had a relationship that turned out to be a trial period — and everyone knows someone who stayed three years in a job that was never going to convert.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
